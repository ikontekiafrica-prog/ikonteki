Frequently Asked Questions About Teki 4 Impakt

Everything you need to know about eligibility, program duration, coaching methodology, and career placement.

Who is eligible to participate in Teki 4 Impakt?

Our fellowships are open to university students, recent IT and computer science graduates, self-taught programmers, and determined youths seeking career transitions into software engineering, web application development, or modern computing roles.

How long is each coaching cohort?

Each intensive cohort spans twelve weeks, divided into foundational coding sprints, real client application builds, code reviews, and dedicated career placement workshops with resume optimization and mock interviews.

Can organizations sponsor student fellowships?

Yes. Through our corporate partnership program, businesses and philanthropic partners sponsor student cohorts, provide real-world project briefs, and receive early access to interview and recruit top-performing graduates.
