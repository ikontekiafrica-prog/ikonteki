Authentic Safari Experiences in Tanzania

IkonTeki Safaris is a Tanzanian-owned safari experience brand that offers curated safari packages across various destinations in Tanzania.

Our Story

A Premier Tour Company

The company focuses on providing authentic, high-quality experiences by connecting travelers with licensed local hosts who are experts in their regions. Guests can browse a selection of thoughtfully designed safari packages that include wildlife safaris, cultural experiences, and luxury accommodations, allowing them to choose trips that match their interests, schedule, and budget.

Why Travel With Us?

Payments and bookings are handled through a secure online platform, ensuring transparency and convenience. The brand emphasizes Tanzanian ownership, fair partnerships with hosts, and a technology-driven approach to streamline the entire travel experience from selection to completion.
