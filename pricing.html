Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

Everything you need to know about our payment structures, delivery guarantees, and project timelines.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, local and international bank wire transfers, and major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard) in both Tanzanian Shillings (TZS) and US Dollars (USD).

How are project payments structured?

Standard development projects require a 50 percent initial deposit to launch engineering sprints, with the remaining 50 percent balance due only upon final client demonstration, milestone approval, and live deployment.

Do I own the source code and digital assets?

Yes, completely. Our core philosophy is "You Own It, We Brand It." Upon final payment, 100 percent of the intellectual property, source code, server credentials, and domain ownership are handed over to your organization.

Do you offer ongoing technical maintenance?

All new deployments include 30 days of complimentary post-launch technical support and staff training. Following that, clients can opt into monthly managed IT and cloud maintenance packages starting from $49 per month.

Need a Bespoke Solution or Custom Scope?

Speak directly with our technical director in Arusha to review your exact software, cloud, or marketing requirements.
