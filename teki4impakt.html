ABOUT TEKI 4 IMPAKT

Empowering Communities Through Practical Technology Education

Technology is transforming the way we live, learn and work. However, many students and young professionals still graduate without the practical digital skills required in today's workplace.

TEKI 4 IMPAKT was established by IkonTeki Africa to bridge this gap through practical learning, career coaching, mentorship, coding workshops and real-world technology experiences.

Our goal is simple — empower communities by transforming learners into innovators, professionals and future leaders.

Our Mission

To empower communities by equipping students, graduates and young professionals with practical digital skills, career readiness and technology-driven opportunities.

Our Vision

To build digitally empowered communities where every young person has the skills, confidence and opportunities to succeed in the digital economy.

OUR PROGRAMS

Building Skills That Create Real Community Impact

Digital Career Coaching

Guiding learners to discover technology careers, strengthen professional confidence and prepare for future opportunities.

Coding & Programming Workshops

Hands-on training in web development, programming, software development and problem-solving using real projects.

Career Readiness Training

Practical workplace skills including CV writing, portfolios, communication, teamwork and interview preparation.

Industry Mentorship

Learn directly from experienced professionals who inspire, mentor and guide participants throughout their career journey.

Technology Bootcamps

Intensive learning experiences designed to help participants build practical projects and gain industry-ready skills.

Innovation & Digital Skills Workshops

Interactive community workshops promoting creativity, innovation, digital literacy and technology awareness.
