IkonTeki Social Impact Initiative

Digital Skills, Coding Mentorship & Career Growth for Youth

Empowering Tanzanian communities through practical software development training, workplace digital literacy, and tech career coaching. We prepare students, graduates, and young innovators with skills that earn a living.

Join a Workshop Partner With Us
Our Mission

To empower communities by equipping students, graduates, and young professionals in Tanzania with practical software engineering skills, career readiness, and technology-driven economic opportunities.

Our Vision

To build digitally empowered communities across East Africa where every young person has the practical skills, confidence, and industry mentorship needed to thrive in the modern digital economy.

Curriculum

Practical Programs for Real Impact

Hands-on, project-based learning led directly by practicing software developers in Arusha.

Digital Career Coaching

Guiding students and graduates to discover technology career paths, build competitive portfolios, and gain the confidence required for modern workplaces.

Coding & Software Workshops

Hands-on coding bootcamps covering HTML, CSS, JavaScript, database design, and real-world software engineering practices.

Workplace Readiness Training

Essential professional skills including technical communication, CV building, GitHub portfolio presentation, and interview preparation.

Direct Industry Mentorship

Learn directly from active software developers who share their production experience, code reviews, and industry insights.

Why We Do It

Bridging the Practical Skills Gap

Many young people in Tanzania graduate with theoretical computer concepts but lack practical experience writing real code, deploying live servers, and collaborating on production software.

Through TEKI 4 IMPAKT, IkonTeki Africa removes this barrier. By giving learners access to mentorship, tools, and real client project exposure, we create a clear pathway from curiosity to sustainable tech employment.
